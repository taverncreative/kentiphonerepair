## Who Fixes iPhone Screens?

A cracked or unresponsive iPhone screen is one of the most common problems smartphone owners face. When it happens to you, the first question is obvious: who do I actually go to for a repair? The answer is not as straightforward as you might think. There are several different types of repair providers, each with their own advantages and drawbacks. In this guide, we break down every option so you can make an informed choice.

### Apple Store and Authorised Service Providers

The most obvious route is to take your iPhone directly to Apple. If you live near an Apple Store or an Apple Authorised Service Provider, you can book a Genius Bar appointment and have the screen replaced using genuine Apple parts.

**Advantages:**
- Genuine Apple parts and components
- Repair carried out by Apple-trained technicians
- Maintains any remaining Apple warranty
- Access to Apple's own diagnostic tools

**Drawbacks:**
- Often requires an appointment days or even weeks in advance
- The nearest Apple Store to most of Kent is Bluewater or London, which means a significant journey
- Pricing is typically at the higher end of the market
- Out-of-warranty repairs can be surprisingly expensive, especially for newer models with OLED displays
- You may need to leave your phone for several hours or even overnight

For many people in Kent, the simple inconvenience of travelling to an Apple Store makes this option impractical, particularly if you rely on your phone for work.

### High Street Third-Party Repair Shops

Most towns across Kent have at least one independent phone repair shop. You will find them in Canterbury, Maidstone, Ashford, Chatham, and many other towns. These shops typically offer walk-in repairs with shorter waiting times than Apple.

**Advantages:**
- Usually cheaper than Apple's own pricing
- Many offer while-you-wait repairs, often within 30 to 60 minutes
- Convenient if you are already in a town centre
- Some shops stock a wide range of parts for older models

**Drawbacks:**
- Quality varies enormously from shop to shop
- Parts quality can be inconsistent, with some shops using lower-grade aftermarket screens
- Not all shops offer warranties on their work
- You still need to travel to the shop and potentially wait around
- Limited accountability if something goes wrong after the repair

The biggest risk with high street shops is inconsistency. Some are excellent and staffed by experienced technicians. Others cut corners to keep prices low, using substandard parts that may fail within weeks. It can be difficult to tell the difference until after the repair is done.

### Mobile Repair Services

Mobile repair services are a newer option that has grown rapidly in popularity across Kent. Instead of you travelling to a shop, a qualified technician comes to your home, workplace, or any convenient location and carries out the repair on the spot.

**Advantages:**
- Maximum convenience, with no need to travel anywhere
- Repairs are typically completed in 30 to 45 minutes at your chosen location
- You can watch the repair being carried out
- Ideal for busy professionals, parents, and anyone with limited mobility
- Personal service and direct communication with the technician
- Often competitively priced compared to both Apple and high street shops

**Drawbacks:**
- Availability depends on the service area of the technician
- Not suitable for highly complex repairs that require specialist workshop equipment

Mobile repair has become increasingly popular across Kent because it removes all the friction from the process. There is no need to find parking in a busy town centre, no waiting around in a shop, and no wasted time in your day. The technician arrives, carries out the repair professionally, and you are back to using your phone almost immediately.

### Mail-In Repair Services

Some companies offer a mail-in service where you post your iPhone to them, they carry out the repair, and post it back. This option is available nationwide regardless of your location.

**Advantages:**
- Available to anyone with a postal address
- Some services offer pre-paid packaging
- Can be a good option for non-urgent repairs

**Drawbacks:**
- You are without your phone for several days, sometimes a week or more
- Risk of damage during transit, even with careful packaging
- No opportunity to discuss the repair face to face
- Returns and disputes can be complicated
- You are trusting your phone to a company you have never met

For most people, being without their phone for days is simply not realistic. Your iPhone contains your communications, banking, two-factor authentication, photos, and more. A mail-in service might work for a secondary device, but for your primary phone, the downtime is usually unacceptable.

### DIY Screen Replacement

With repair kits available online, some people consider replacing their own iPhone screen. While this is technically possible, it comes with significant risks.

**Advantages:**
- Potentially the cheapest option
- Satisfying if you enjoy hands-on technical work

**Drawbacks:**
- Requires specialist tools and a steady hand
- Risk of damaging internal components, particularly ribbon cables
- Newer iPhones have components that require software pairing, such as Face ID modules and True Tone calibration
- Voids any remaining warranty
- No guarantee of a successful outcome
- If something goes wrong, professional repair becomes more expensive

Unless you have genuine experience with electronics repair, a DIY screen replacement is rarely worth the risk. The potential savings are small compared to the cost of making the problem worse.

### Which Option Is Best?

The right choice depends on your priorities. If maintaining your Apple warranty is paramount and cost is not a concern, the Apple Store is the safest bet. If you want a balance of convenience, quality, and fair pricing, a reputable mobile repair service is hard to beat.

For most people across Kent, a mobile repair service offers the best overall experience. You get a professional repair carried out at your convenience, using quality parts, with a warranty on the work, and without any of the hassle of travelling to a shop or posting your phone away.
