### Common iPhone Repairs in Faversham

We carry out the full range of iPhone repairs for Faversham customers. The most common issues we see include:

**Screen replacements** are by far our most requested repair. Whether you have dropped your phone in the car park at Tesco, cracked it walking along the creek, or your child has thrown it onto the kitchen floor, we can replace the screen on the spot. We carry screens for all iPhone models from the iPhone 6 through to the latest iPhone 16 range, so there is no need to wait for parts to be ordered.

**Battery replacements** are the second most common repair. If your iPhone is not lasting through the day, shutting down unexpectedly, or showing a degraded battery health reading, a new battery can restore it to its original performance. This is a quick repair that typically takes around 20 to 30 minutes.

**Charging port repairs** are needed when your iPhone will not charge reliably, when the cable feels loose in the port, or when charging only works at certain angles. Sometimes a thorough clean of the port resolves the issue, but if the port itself is damaged, we can replace it.

**Camera repairs** for both the front and rear cameras are also available. Blurry photos, a cracked camera lens, or a camera that will not focus can all be addressed.
