## Can You Fix a Cracked Phone Screen?

The short answer is yes, in almost every case a cracked phone screen can be repaired. The longer answer depends on the type of damage, the model of your phone, and who carries out the repair. A cracked screen is the most common smartphone repair worldwide, and modern repair techniques mean you can have your phone looking and functioning like new again, often within the same day.

## Types of Screen Damage

Not all cracks are created equal. Understanding the type of damage you are dealing with helps set expectations for the repair.

### Surface glass cracks

This is the most common type. The top glass layer is cracked, but the display underneath still works perfectly. You can see everything clearly, touch response is normal, and there are no discoloured patches. These are typically the simplest and most affordable repairs.

### LCD or OLED damage

If you see black spots, coloured lines, flickering, or areas where the display does not respond to touch, the damage has gone beyond the glass and into the display panel itself. This requires a full screen assembly replacement rather than just a glass swap, which increases the cost but is still very much repairable.

### Shattered screens

Severe impacts can leave the screen in pieces, with chunks of glass missing or the entire surface spiderwebbed beyond recognition. Even in these cases, the phone can be repaired. The screen assembly is replaced entirely, and the phone returns to full function.

### Digitiser damage

Sometimes the glass looks fine but touch response is erratic, unresponsive in certain areas, or registers phantom touches. This indicates damage to the digitiser layer, which is responsible for detecting your finger input. A screen replacement resolves this issue.

## DIY Repair vs Professional Repair

The internet is full of videos showing people replacing their own iPhone screens on a kitchen table. While it is technically possible, there are serious risks to consider.

### Risks of DIY screen repair

- **Voiding any remaining warranty.** Opening your phone yourself will void Apple's warranty and any AppleCare coverage.
- **Damaging internal components.** iPhones are packed with delicate ribbon cables, sensors, and connectors. One slip can damage the front camera, Face ID module, earpiece speaker, or proximity sensor.
- **Waterproofing loss.** Professional repairs include reapplying adhesive seals. DIY repairs almost never restore the original water resistance.
- **Poor quality parts.** Screens sold to consumers online are often lower quality than those available to professional repair services. Colours may look washed out, touch response may be less accurate, and brightness may be reduced.
- **No warranty on your work.** If something goes wrong during or after a DIY repair, you are on your own.

### Benefits of professional repair

- Trained technicians who handle these repairs daily.
- Access to high-quality replacement screens with accurate colour reproduction and full touch sensitivity.
- Proper tools designed for phone repair, not improvised alternatives.
- A warranty on both parts and labour.
- Correct reinstallation of all internal components, including waterproof seals where applicable.

## What Happens If You Ignore a Cracked Screen?

It can be tempting to carry on using a cracked screen, especially if it is a small crack that does not seem to affect usability. However, leaving a cracked screen unrepaired carries real risks.

- **Cuts and injuries.** Cracked glass has sharp edges. Running your finger across a crack repeatedly can cause cuts, and holding the phone to your face during calls puts broken glass against your skin.
- **Moisture and dust ingress.** Cracks create entry points for moisture and dust to reach the internal components. This can cause corrosion, short circuits, and further damage that is far more expensive to repair than the original screen.
- **Worsening damage.** Cracks spread over time, especially with temperature changes and daily handling. A small crack today can become a shattered screen next week.
- **Reduced resale value.** If you plan to sell or trade in your phone, a cracked screen dramatically reduces its value. The cost of repair is often recouped through the higher resale price.

## Cost Considerations

Screen repair costs vary depending on two main factors: the phone model and the quality of the replacement screen.

### What affects the price

- **Phone model.** Newer and larger iPhones generally cost more to repair because the screens themselves are more expensive. An iPhone SE screen replacement costs significantly less than an iPhone 15 Pro Max.
- **Screen quality.** There is a meaningful difference between budget aftermarket screens and high-quality replacements that match the original specifications. At Kent iPhone Repair, we use quality screens that maintain accurate colours, brightness, and touch sensitivity.
- **Type of damage.** Simple glass-only damage may cost less than full display assembly replacement, depending on the model and repair approach.

### Comparing your options

- **Apple Store repair** tends to be the most expensive option, especially without AppleCare. You also need to book an appointment, travel to the nearest store, and potentially leave your phone for several days.
- **High street repair shops** vary enormously in quality and price. Some use excellent parts and employ skilled technicians. Others cut corners with cheap components and inexperienced staff.
