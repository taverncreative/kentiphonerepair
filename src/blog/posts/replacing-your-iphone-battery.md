## Replacing Your iPhone Battery

Every iPhone battery degrades over time. It is an unavoidable characteristic of lithium-ion chemistry: each charge cycle slightly reduces the battery's maximum capacity. After a couple of years of daily use, most people start noticing that their phone does not last as long as it used to. The good news is that a battery replacement is one of the quickest, most affordable, and most transformative repairs you can have done. It is like giving your phone a completely new lease of life.

### When to Replace Your Battery

Not sure whether your battery actually needs replacing? Here are the key indicators:

**Check your Battery Health.** Go to Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health and Charging. You will see a Maximum Capacity percentage. When new, your battery is at 100 percent. Apple considers a battery to be consumed when it drops below 80 percent.

- **90 to 100 percent:** Your battery is in good health. No replacement needed.
- **80 to 90 percent:** You may notice some reduced stamina, particularly with heavy use. Replacement is optional but can make a noticeable difference.
- **Below 80 percent:** Replacement is recommended. You will likely be experiencing significant battery life reduction and possibly unexpected shutdowns.

**Practical signs your battery needs replacing:**
- Your phone does not last through a normal day without needing a charge
- The battery percentage drops rapidly, sometimes jumping from 30 percent to 10 percent or lower in minutes
- The phone shuts down unexpectedly even when it shows charge remaining
- The phone gets unusually warm during normal use or while charging
- The phone performs noticeably slower (iOS throttles performance when the battery is degraded to prevent shutdowns)
- You see a Service message in the Battery Health settings

**Physical signs of battery failure:**
- The screen appears to be lifting slightly from the body, which can indicate a swelling battery
- A visible bulge on the back of the phone
- Any deformation of the phone's case

If you notice any physical signs, stop using the phone and get it assessed immediately. A swollen battery is a safety hazard and should not be ignored.

### What to Expect from a Battery Replacement

If you have never had a battery replaced, here is what the process involves:

1. **Diagnosis confirmation.** The technician verifies the battery health reading and discusses the symptoms with you to confirm that a new battery is the right solution.

2. **Powering down.** The phone is completely powered off before any work begins.

3. **Opening the phone.** The technician uses specialist tools to carefully remove the screen from the body of the phone. This requires precision to avoid damaging the screen's ribbon cables.

4. **Disconnecting the old battery.** The battery connector is detached from the logic board. The battery itself is held in place with adhesive pull tabs. These tabs are carefully pulled to release the battery without bending or puncturing it.

5. **Installing the new battery.** A new battery with the correct capacity for your model is positioned and secured with fresh adhesive. The connector is reattached to the logic board.

6. **Reassembly.** The screen is reconnected and the phone is sealed back together with fresh adhesive to maintain dust and moisture resistance.

7. **Testing.** The phone is powered on and tested to ensure the new battery is functioning correctly, charging properly, and being recognised by iOS.

The entire process typically takes 20 to 30 minutes for an experienced technician.

### Cost of Battery Replacement

Battery replacement is one of the most affordable iPhone repairs. The cost varies depending on the model, with newer and larger phones generally being slightly more expensive due to higher-capacity batteries. However, even for the latest models, a battery replacement is a fraction of the cost of a new phone.

Apple offers battery replacement through their stores and authorised providers, but their pricing is higher than independent repair services, and availability can be limited, often requiring an appointment days or weeks in advance.

### Performance Improvement

Many people are surprised by just how much difference a new battery makes. The improvements go beyond simply lasting longer:

**Restored battery life.** The most obvious benefit. A new battery takes you back to the stamina you had when the phone was new, typically lasting a full day of normal use on a single charge.

**Restored performance.** Since iOS 11.3, Apple has included a performance management feature that throttles the processor speed when the battery is degraded. This prevents unexpected shutdowns but makes the phone feel sluggish. A new battery removes this throttling, and the phone feels noticeably faster.

**Reliable percentage readings.** An old battery often gives inaccurate charge readings, jumping from one percentage to another unpredictably. A new battery provides smooth, reliable charge indication.

**No more unexpected shutdowns.** A degraded battery cannot deliver sufficient power during peak demand moments, causing the phone to shut down. A new battery eliminates this problem.

**Reduced heat.** A degraded battery often generates more heat during use and charging. A new battery runs cooler.

### Aftercare Tips

To get the most life out of your new battery:

- **Enable Optimised Battery Charging** in Settings. This feature learns your routine and avoids holding the battery at 100 percent for extended periods, which reduces wear.
- **Avoid extreme temperatures.** Do not leave your phone in a hot car or use it in freezing conditions for extended periods.
- **Use a quality charger.** Cheap, uncertified chargers can affect battery health. Use Apple's charger or a reputable MFi-certified alternative.
- **Do not routinely drain to zero.** Lithium-ion batteries prefer to stay between 20 and 80 percent most of the time.
- **Keep iOS updated.** Apple regularly includes battery optimisation improvements in iOS updates.

With proper care, a replacement battery should last two to three years before showing significant degradation.

### Is It Worth Replacing on an Older Phone?

This is a common question. If your phone is several years old, is a battery replacement still worthwhile? In most cases, yes.

If the phone still runs the current version of iOS, performs adequately for your needs, and the only issue is battery life, a replacement battery transforms the experience for a very modest cost. It is far more cost-effective than buying a new phone, and it keeps a perfectly functional device out of the waste stream.

The only scenario where it might not be worthwhile is if the phone is no longer receiving iOS updates and you also need other repairs. In that case, the combined cost of repairs on an end-of-life device may not represent good value.
