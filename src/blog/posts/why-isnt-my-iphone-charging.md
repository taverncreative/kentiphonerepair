## Why Isn't My iPhone Charging?

Few things are more frustrating than plugging in your iPhone only to find it refuses to charge. Whether the battery icon stays stubbornly red or nothing happens at all, a non-charging iPhone can quickly derail your day. Before you panic, though, there are several common reasons this happens, and many of them have straightforward fixes you can try at home.

At Kent iPhone Repair, we see charging issues every single week across Ashford, Maidstone, Canterbury, Folkestone, and the wider Kent area. Here is a detailed look at the most common causes and what you can do about each one.

## Dirty or Blocked Charging Port

This is the single most common reason an iPhone stops charging, and it is also the easiest to fix. Over time, pocket lint, dust, and debris accumulate inside the Lightning or USB-C port. That build-up prevents the charging cable from making a solid connection with the internal contacts.

### How to check and clean it

- Power off your iPhone completely before attempting any cleaning.
- Use a wooden or plastic toothpick to gently scrape out any visible debris. Never use a metal tool, as this can damage the delicate pins inside the port.
- A can of compressed air held at an angle can help dislodge stubborn particles, but avoid blasting air directly into the port at close range.
- Shine a torch into the port to check your work. You should be able to see clean, shiny contacts at the back.

If cleaning the port resolves the issue, you have saved yourself a trip to a repair service. If it does not, read on.

## Faulty or Damaged Charging Cable

Cables take a beating. They get bent, pulled, stepped on, and shoved into bags day after day. Even if a cable looks fine on the outside, the internal wiring can fray or break, especially near the connector ends.

### Quick tests to rule out the cable

- Try a different cable that you know works with another device.
- Inspect the connector for bent or blackened pins.
- Check for fraying or kinks along the cable, particularly where it meets the connector.
- If you are using a third-party cable, make sure it is MFi-certified. Non-certified cables can cause charging problems and may even damage your iPhone over time.

If a different cable charges your phone without issue, the old cable was the culprit. Replace it and move on.

## Software Glitches and Frozen Processes

Sometimes your iPhone's software gets into a state where it simply will not recognise a charger, even though there is nothing physically wrong.

### Software troubleshooting steps

- **Force restart your iPhone.** On iPhone 8 and later, quickly press and release the Volume Up button, then the Volume Down button, then press and hold the Side button until you see the Apple logo. This clears temporary glitches without affecting your data.
- **Check for iOS updates.** Go to Settings > General > Software Update. Apple occasionally releases patches that fix charging-related bugs.
- **Reset all settings.** Go to Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset All Settings. This will not erase your data but will reset Wi-Fi passwords and display preferences. It can resolve stubborn software conflicts.

## Battery Degradation

Every lithium-ion battery has a limited lifespan. After roughly 500 full charge cycles, your iPhone battery retains about 80% of its original capacity, and performance starts to decline noticeably.

### Signs your battery is degrading

- The battery percentage drops rapidly even during light use.
- Your iPhone shuts down unexpectedly, often at 15-20% battery.
- The phone gets unusually hot during charging.
- Charging takes significantly longer than it used to.

You can check your battery health by going to Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. If the Maximum Capacity is below 80%, Apple considers the battery "consumed" and replacement is recommended.

## Water Damage

Modern iPhones have impressive water resistance ratings, but no phone is truly waterproof. Water resistance degrades over time, especially if the phone has been dropped or the seals have been compromised.

### Warning signs of moisture damage

- A "Liquid Detected" warning when you plug in the charger.
- Corrosion visible inside the charging port (green or white residue).
- The phone charges intermittently or only at certain angles.

If you suspect water damage, do not attempt to charge the phone. Sending electrical current through a wet circuit can cause further damage. Instead, let the phone dry thoroughly and seek professional assessment.

## Hardware Failure

If none of the above steps resolve your charging issue, you may be dealing with a hardware fault. Common hardware culprits include:

- **A damaged charging port assembly.** The port itself can crack or come loose from the logic board, especially after a drop.
- **A failing battery.** Beyond general degradation, batteries can develop internal faults that prevent them from accepting a charge.
- **Logic board issues.** In rare cases, the charging IC (integrated circuit) on the logic board can fail, requiring micro-soldering repair.

Hardware problems require professional diagnosis. Guessing and replacing parts without proper testing can be expensive and counterproductive.
