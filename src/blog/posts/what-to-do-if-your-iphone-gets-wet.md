## What to Do If Your iPhone Gets Wet

<time class="blog-post__date" datetime="2026-02-17">17 February 2026</time>
</div>

<div class="blog-post__content">

Water and iPhones do not mix well, despite what the marketing might suggest. While newer iPhone models carry IP68 water resistance ratings, these ratings degrade over time and do not cover all types of liquid exposure. If your iPhone has come into contact with water, acting quickly can make the difference between a recoverable device and a costly replacement.

## Immediate Steps to Take

The first few minutes after water exposure are critical:

1. **Remove the phone from water immediately**
2. **Power it off** — hold the side button and slide to power off. Do not try to use it
3. **Remove the case** — take off any case to allow air to reach the device
4. **Dry the exterior** — use a lint-free cloth to gently pat dry every surface
5. **Eject the SIM tray** — use the SIM tool to open the tray and let air in
6. **Place in a dry, warm area** — set it somewhere with good air circulation

## What NOT to Do

- **Do not put it in rice** — rice does not effectively absorb moisture from inside a sealed device and can introduce dust into ports
- **Do not use a hairdryer** — heat can damage internal components and push moisture deeper
- **Do not charge it** — connecting a wet phone to power can cause a short circuit
- **Do not press buttons repeatedly** — this can push water further into the device
- **Do not shake it vigorously** — this can spread water to areas it had not reached

## Understanding Water Resistance Ratings

Modern iPhones carry an IP68 rating, but important caveats exist:

- The rating degrades over time through normal use, drops, and temperature changes
- It does not cover salt water, chlorinated water, or soapy water
- It does not cover water pressure from jets, taps, or waves
- Apple does not cover water damage under warranty, even with the IP68 rating

## Signs of Water Damage

Watch for these warning signs after exposure:

- Screen flickering, discolouration, or condensation under the glass
- Muffled or distorted audio from speakers
- Charging issues or liquid detected warnings
- Touchscreen becoming unresponsive or phantom touches
- Overheating during normal use
- Camera fog or blurring
- Unexpected shutdowns or restart loops

## When to Seek Professional Help

If your iPhone shows any symptoms, or was submerged for more than a few seconds, professional assessment is recommended. Internal corrosion develops over hours or days, even if the phone seems fine initially.

A qualified technician can open the device, inspect for corrosion, clean affected components with specialist solutions, and replace damaged parts before the damage spreads.

## Prevention Going Forward

- Use a waterproof pouch near pools or beaches
- Avoid using your phone in the bath or shower
- Keep drinks away from your phone on desks
- Consider a case with raised edges for splash protection
