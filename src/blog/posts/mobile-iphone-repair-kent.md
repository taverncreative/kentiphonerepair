## The Mobile Repair Model: How It Works

The concept is simple but transformative. Instead of you going to a repair shop, a trained iPhone technician comes to you. The technician arrives at your home, workplace, school, cafe, or any other location with everything needed to complete the repair on site. No shop front, no waiting room, no parking hassles. Just a professional repair at a time and place that suits your life.

### Why mobile repair makes sense

Traditional repair shops have overhead costs: rent, rates, utilities, shop fitting, and staff to manage the counter. These costs are built into the price you pay. A mobile repair service eliminates much of this overhead, which means competitive pricing for you without any compromise on quality.

More importantly, mobile repair eliminates the single biggest pain point of traditional repair: the disruption to your day. Instead of blocking out two or three hours to travel, wait, and collect, you simply answer the door or meet the technician at an agreed location. The repair happens while you carry on with your morning, your lunch break, or your evening.

## What We Repair

Our mobile service covers the complete range of common iPhone repairs.

### Screen repairs

From hairline cracks to completely shattered displays, we replace screens with quality parts that match the original for colour accuracy, brightness, and touch response. Most screen repairs are completed in 30 to 45 minutes. We handle all iPhone models from the iPhone 6 through to the latest iPhone 16 range.

### Battery replacements

A degraded battery affects everything: battery life, performance (through iOS throttling), and reliability. We replace batteries with quality cells that restore your iPhone to like-new battery performance. The repair takes around 20 to 30 minutes, and you will notice the difference immediately.

### Charging port repairs

If your iPhone will not charge reliably, only charges at certain angles, or has stopped charging entirely, the charging port assembly likely needs replacing. We fit new port assemblies that restore reliable charging and data connectivity. This covers both Lightning and USB-C models.

### Camera repairs

Whether it is a cracked camera lens, a blurry rear camera, or a faulty front-facing camera, we can replace camera modules on site. We stock parts for both front and rear cameras across the popular iPhone range.

### Other repairs

We also handle:

- Earpiece and loudspeaker replacements
- Power button and volume button repairs
- Mute switch repairs
- Rear glass replacements (on applicable models)
- Various other hardware faults

If you are not sure whether your issue is something we can fix, just get in touch. We will give you an honest answer.
