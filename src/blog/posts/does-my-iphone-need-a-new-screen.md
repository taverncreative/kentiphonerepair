## Does My iPhone Need a New Screen?

It is not always obvious whether your iPhone screen actually needs replacing. Sometimes the signs are unmistakable, like a shattered display with half the screen blacked out. Other times, the symptoms are more subtle, and you might wonder whether you are overreacting or whether there is genuinely a problem that needs attention. This guide will help you assess your screen and determine whether a replacement is necessary.

### Cosmetic Damage: Cracks and Scratches

The most visible sign of screen damage is cracking or scratching. But does cosmetic damage always mean you need a new screen?

**Hairline cracks.** A single hairline crack with no effect on display quality or touch response is technically cosmetic. The phone works fine, and you might be tempted to ignore it. However, even a hairline crack compromises the phone's water resistance and can spread over time. It is worth addressing before it becomes a bigger problem.

**Spider web cracks.** Multiple cracks radiating from an impact point are more serious. While the phone may still function, the structural integrity of the screen is significantly reduced. The glass is weaker and more likely to shatter completely with the next drop. Additionally, the cracks create entry points for moisture and debris.

**Deep scratches.** Scratches on the glass surface are usually cosmetic and do not affect functionality. However, deep scratches that you can feel with your fingernail may eventually affect the oleophobic coating and make the screen less pleasant to use.

**Missing glass fragments.** If pieces of glass have actually separated from the screen, replacement is essential. Exposed edges are dangerous, and the gaps allow contaminants into the phone.

**The verdict:** Minor cosmetic damage is tolerable in the short term, but any crack, no matter how small, is a reason to consider replacement. The longer you leave it, the greater the risk of the damage worsening.

### Touch Issues

Touch problems are a clear sign that your screen needs attention. Here are the specific symptoms to watch for:

**Unresponsive areas.** If tapping in certain areas of the screen produces no response, the digitiser in that region is damaged. You can test this by opening the Notes app and trying to draw across the entire screen. Any areas where your drawing line disappears indicate a dead zone.

**Ghost touches.** If your phone registers touches that you are not making, this is known as ghost touching. You might notice apps opening by themselves, text being typed without your input, or settings changing randomly. Ghost touches are caused by a malfunctioning digitiser and will only get worse over time.

**Delayed response.** If there is a noticeable lag between touching the screen and the phone responding, the digitiser may be partially damaged. This is different from general phone slowness, which is usually a software or processor issue.

**Inconsistent sensitivity.** If some areas of the screen require much harder pressing than others to register a touch, the digitiser layer is failing unevenly. This is often a precursor to complete failure in those areas.

**The verdict:** Any persistent touch issue is a strong indicator that you need a screen replacement. Touch problems almost never resolve themselves and typically get worse.

### Display Problems

Display issues are separate from touch issues and relate to what you see on screen rather than how the screen responds to your input.

**Black spots or blotches.** Dark areas that do not display any content usually indicate damage to the display panel itself. On LCD screens, this means the liquid crystal layer has been disrupted. On OLED screens, it means pixels have been damaged or have died.

**Coloured lines.** Vertical or horizontal lines of colour across the screen are a classic sign of display damage. These can be caused by a cracked display panel, a damaged ribbon cable, or a loose connector. A technician can determine which.

**Flickering.** If the screen brightness fluctuates randomly or the display flickers on and off, there may be damage to the display panel or the connection between the screen and the logic board.

**Colour distortion.** If colours look wrong, with a green, pink, or yellow tint that was not there before, the display panel is likely damaged. This is particularly common on OLED screens after impact damage.

**Burn-in.** On OLED screens, static images displayed for very long periods can leave a permanent ghost image. While not a functional issue initially, severe burn-in can be distracting and is irreversible without a screen replacement.

**Reduced brightness.** If your screen no longer reaches the brightness it once did, or if one area is noticeably dimmer than the rest, the display panel may be deteriorating.

**The verdict:** Display problems almost always require a screen replacement. Unlike touch issues, which can sometimes be caused by a loose connector, display damage typically means the panel itself needs replacing.

### When It Might Not Be the Screen

Sometimes what appears to be a screen problem is actually caused by something else entirely:

- **Software glitches** can cause the screen to freeze, become unresponsive, or display incorrectly. A force restart or software update may resolve the issue.
- **A loose connector** can cause intermittent display or touch issues. A technician can open the phone and reseat the connection without replacing the screen.
- **Water damage** can cause touch and display issues that mimic screen damage but are actually caused by corrosion on the logic board.
- **A failing battery** can cause the phone to behave erratically, including screen issues, particularly when the battery is at low charge.

A professional assessment can determine whether the screen itself is the issue or whether the problem lies elsewhere.

### The Bottom Line

If you are experiencing any of the following, your iPhone likely needs a new screen:

- Visible cracks or missing glass
- Unresponsive touch areas
- Ghost touches
- Black spots, lines, or colour distortion
- Flickering or inconsistent brightness

If you are unsure, the best course of action is to get a professional opinion. A good technician can diagnose the problem quickly and give you an honest assessment of whether a screen replacement is needed.
