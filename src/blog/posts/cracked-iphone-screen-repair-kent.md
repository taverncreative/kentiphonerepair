## Screen Quality Matters

Not all replacement screens are the same. There is a wide range of quality in the aftermarket screen market, and the screen fitted to your iPhone directly affects your daily experience.

### What to look for in a quality screen

- **Accurate colour reproduction.** A good replacement screen should display colours that match or closely approximate the original. Cheap screens often look washed out, overly warm, or noticeably different from the original.
- **Proper brightness levels.** Lower-quality screens may not reach the same peak brightness as the original, making them harder to read in sunlight.
- **Responsive touch input.** The digitiser layer must register touches accurately and consistently. Cheap screens sometimes have dead zones or delayed response.
- **Oleophobic coating.** Original iPhone screens have a fingerprint-resistant coating. Quality replacements include this coating, while budget options often skip it, leaving you with a screen that smudges constantly.

At Kent iPhone Repair, we use carefully sourced screens that meet high standards for colour accuracy, brightness, touch response, and durability. We do not cut corners on parts because we know you will be looking at and touching this screen thousands of times a day.

## Common Questions About Screen Repair

**Will my data be safe during the repair?**
Absolutely. A screen replacement does not affect your data, photos, messages, or apps. Everything stays exactly as it is.

**Do I need to back up my phone before a screen repair?**
While a screen repair should not affect your data, we always recommend having a recent backup as general good practice. If you need help with this, we are happy to guide you through the process.

**Will my phone still be water-resistant after the repair?**
We apply adhesive seals during the repair process. While we restore the sealing to a high standard, it is worth noting that no third-party repair can guarantee the same level of water resistance as the original factory seal. We always advise treating your phone with care around water regardless.

**How long does the repair take?**
Most screen repairs are completed in 30 to 45 minutes. Complex cases or older models may take slightly longer, but we will always let you know the expected timeframe upfront.
