## How to Fix a Cracked iPhone Screen

A cracked iPhone screen is more than a cosmetic annoyance. It affects usability, puts your fingers at risk of cuts, and exposes your phone's internals to dust and moisture. The good news is that screen repair is one of the most common and well-understood smartphone repairs available, and there are several routes to getting your phone back to its best.

## Step 1: Assess the Damage

Before deciding on a repair approach, take a careful look at the damage. The severity determines the type of repair needed and affects the cost.

### Glass-only damage

If the glass is cracked but the display underneath works perfectly, this is the best-case scenario. You can still see everything clearly, colours look normal, there are no dark spots or lines, and touch response works across the entire screen. This type of damage is typically the most affordable to repair.

### Display damage

If you see any of the following, the damage extends beyond the glass into the LCD or OLED display panel:

- Black or dark spots on the screen
- Coloured lines running horizontally or vertically
- Flickering or flashing display
- Areas where the screen does not respond to touch
- Colour distortion or a greenish/pinkish tint in certain areas

Display damage requires a full screen assembly replacement, which costs more but is still very much repairable.

### Touch and digitiser issues

Sometimes the glass may have minimal visible damage, but touch response is compromised. This can manifest as:

- Dead zones where touches are not registered
- Ghost touches where the phone registers inputs you did not make
- Delayed or inaccurate touch response
- The phone dialling numbers or opening apps on its own

These symptoms indicate digitiser damage and require a screen replacement.

## Step 2: Apply Temporary Protection

If you cannot get the screen repaired immediately, take these steps to protect yourself and your phone in the interim.

- **Apply a screen protector over the crack.** A tempered glass screen protector placed over the cracked screen holds the broken glass together, prevents further splintering, and protects your fingers from sharp edges. It is not a permanent fix, but it buys you time safely.
- **Use a case with raised edges.** A case with a raised lip around the screen provides a buffer that prevents the cracked screen from making direct contact with surfaces when placed face down.
- **Avoid moisture.** A cracked screen has compromised the water resistance of your phone. Be extra careful around water, rain, and humid environments until the screen is repaired.

## Step 3: Understand Your Repair Options

### Apple Store or authorised service provider

**Pros:** Genuine Apple parts, Apple-trained technicians, maintains any remaining warranty.

**Cons:** Typically the most expensive option, especially without AppleCare+. The nearest Apple Store to many Kent locations involves a significant journey. Appointments may not be immediately available, and some repairs require leaving the phone for a day or more.

### Independent repair shop

**Pros:** Generally more affordable than Apple. No appointment needed at many shops. Parts quality can be very good at reputable shops.

**Cons:** Quality varies enormously between shops. Some use excellent parts and employ skilled technicians, while others use the cheapest available components and cut corners. You need to visit the shop, which means finding parking and potentially waiting around.

### Mobile repair service

**Pros:** Maximum convenience as the technician comes to your chosen location. Competitive pricing. Personal, face-to-face service. No travel or waiting at a shop.

**Cons:** Availability depends on the service covering your area. (Kent iPhone Repair covers the entire county, so this is not an issue for Kent residents.)

**Pros:** Lowest upfront cost for parts. Sense of accomplishment if successful.

## The Risks of DIY Screen Repair

DIY screen repair tutorials make it look straightforward, but there is a substantial gap between watching a video and actually performing the repair successfully. Here are the real risks.

### Damaging Face ID permanently

On iPhones with Face ID (iPhone X and later), the front camera assembly includes the TrueDepth sensor system that makes Face ID work. This component is paired to the logic board at the factory, and if it is damaged during a screen replacement, Face ID cannot be restored. Even Apple cannot fix it without replacing the entire phone. The ribbon cable connecting this module is extremely delicate and easy to tear.

### Breaking internal ribbon cables

iPhones contain multiple ribbon cables that connect the screen assembly to the logic board. These cables are thin, fragile, and positioned in ways that make them easy to snag or tear during removal and reinstallation. Damaging these cables can disable the front camera, earpiece speaker, proximity sensor, or ambient light sensor.

### Losing water resistance

iPhones use a specific adhesive seal between the screen and the frame that provides water resistance. Properly removing and reapplying this seal requires the correct adhesive and technique. Most DIY repairs skip this step entirely, leaving the phone vulnerable to moisture damage.

### Screen quality issues

Screens available to consumers online are typically lower quality than those supplied to professional repair services. Common issues include reduced brightness, inaccurate colours, poor touch sensitivity, and missing oleophobic coating.

### No warranty or recourse

If a DIY repair goes wrong, you have no warranty to fall back on. If you damage an additional component during the process, you are now facing a more expensive professional repair than the original screen replacement would have been.

## Cost Factors

Screen repair costs depend on several variables.

- **iPhone model.** Newer models with larger, more advanced displays cost more to repair. An iPhone SE screen costs considerably less than an iPhone 15 Pro Max.
- **Type of screen.** OLED screens (used in Pro models and iPhone X onwards) cost more to replace than LCD screens (used in standard models of the iPhone 11 and earlier).
- **Parts quality.** There is a genuine cost difference between budget aftermarket screens and high-quality replacements that match the original specifications.
- **Labour and warranty.** Professional repairs include the labour of a trained technician and a warranty on parts and workmanship.
